Why the cage?

Lightning stays outside

A Faraday cage is a box of metal bars that keeps electric charge on the outside. Strike it with lightning and whoever is inside stays safe. That's the idea behind the name and the striped square in the logo.

The café sits in a former warehouse on Gore Street that was once home to the Adelphia recording studio. Today it's brunch, coffee and bread from the bakery.
