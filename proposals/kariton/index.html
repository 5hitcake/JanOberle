Kariton means cart

From the cart to Footscray

In the Philippines, ice cream comes from the kariton, the colourful cart with a bell that rolls through every neighbourhood. Kariton started in 2020, during lockdown, as a love letter to those carts.

Today it's a shop on Leeds Street in Footscray, a cabinet of rotating Filipino flavours, and pop-ups and festivals from Melbourne to Sydney.
