Art on the walls

Our walls are a gallery for local artists, and most of the work is for sale. We've just had a glow up, with a brand-new exhibition to go with it.

Past shows include “Southside Scenes” by Frank Boyle and “The Magical Realms” with Moe Rocksmoore, Gilly Ferns and Ashley Cook, with the bar open until 11pm on opening night.
