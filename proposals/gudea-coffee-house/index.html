Who is Gudea

A ruler who built temples

Gudea was a ruler of Lagash, a Sumerian city-state in southern Mesopotamia, in the late 22nd century BCE. He is remembered for his great building projects, above all the temple of Ningirsu, the city's patron god.

His statues and inscriptions speak of piety, justice and prosperity for his people, and his time is known for its art and its peace. His face is our logo, and his name is on our door at 121 St George's Road.
