    About

    Raised on night buses and borrowed beats

    Saint Marlo grew up writing verses on the last bus home and recording them on a laptop mic in his cousin's closet. Ten years later the closet is a studio, but the stories still come from the same streets.

    Concrete Gospel is his debut album: boom bap and trap side by side, with hooks for the crowd and verses for the people who listen twice.

    “Every line sounds like it was written under a streetlight.”
