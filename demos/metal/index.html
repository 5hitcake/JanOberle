    The band

    Born in a basement, built for big rooms

    Vrakhold started as four friends, one borrowed amp and a rehearsal room with no windows. Three records later the amp is theirs, the rooms got bigger, and the songs got slower and heavier where it counts.

    Ashen Crown is their most direct album yet: doom-paced verses that break into thrash, and choruses written to be shouted back.
